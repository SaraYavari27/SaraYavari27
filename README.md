## Hi there 👋

# Sara Yavari

### Ph.D. Candidate in Computer Science | Medical Imaging and Artificial Intelligence Researcher

I am a Ph.D. candidate at **DePaul University** specializing in artificial intelligence, computer vision, and medical image analysis. My research focuses on applying **artificial intelligence to image and text data** , with particular interests in medical image analysis and natural language processing for healthcare. Drawing on my background in cybersecurity and telecommunications, I also apply AI techniques to network security, intrusion detection, and anomaly detection.

My research explores diffusion models, generative AI, vision transformers, large language models, knowledge distillation, continual learning, and deep neural networks for analyzing MRI, CT, ultrasound, and other biomedical imaging modalities.

I am particularly interested in medical image segmentation, classification, registration, anomaly detection, multimodal learning, large language models (LLMs), natural language processing (NLP), text analysis, vision-language models, and AI-assisted analysis of healthcare data.

I welcome research collaborations and academic opportunities in artificial intelligence, computer vision, medical imaging, healthcare NLP, and interdisciplinary healthcare AI.
